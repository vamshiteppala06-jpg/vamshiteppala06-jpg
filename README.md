I'm a final-year **Electrical & Electronics Engineering** student who builds AI-powered products, not just studies textbooks. Currently focused on solving real problems for Indian freshers through **[Kanyarasi.ai](https://kanyarasi.ai)** — a mock interview and job-matching platform for Tier 2 & 3 cities.

- 🎓 **B.Tech EEE** @ Avanthi Institute of Engineering & Technology, Visakhapatnam
- 🤖 Building **Kanyarasi.ai** — AI mock interviews for Indian engineering freshers
- 🧠 Learning by doing: Python, GenAI, Prompt Engineering

**A few things about me:**
